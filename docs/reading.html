Deep reading

A deep reading takes a few pages of a source and checks every assertion on them: the quotation is verified against the transcription, every number is recomputed, every geometrical statement is a proposition of a model or a step of a derivation, and every result comes with its evidence — the crop of the page. The record is a YAML file (readings/<name>.yaml), the report is reproducible (famulus-astra reading readings/<name>.yaml), and every item points back to its paragraph.

An item:

- id: battani-advance
  locus: fr:kepler-sec/Swerdlow2026/0052/p06
  quote: "λA = 82;17°, a notable advance of 16;37° in 743 years"
  kind: computation
  formal: "82;17 - 65;30"
  expect: "16;37"
  places: 1
  check_of: print
  evidence: {page: 52, box: [0.10, 0.855, 0.92, 0.895]}

formal is sexagesimal arithmetic or one of

form what is checked
proposition <model>: <name> a proposition stated in a model holds over its parameter space and states
step <derivation>: <name> a step (or conclusion) of a derivation is true and justified
computation <derivation>: <name> a computed or determined quantity agrees with the printed one
sign <model>[<parametrisation>]: <observable> a sign rule, read off the model
solve <model>[<parametrisation>]: <obs> at <obs2> = <angle> a value in the state where another observable has a value

check_of distinguishes the author’s own statements (print) from historical figures the author reports (reported): a discrepancy in the first is about the page, in the second about the source reported. A quotation that is not in the paragraph’s transcription fails the run.

The two discrepancies of the print are misprints: the sign rule for the equation of centre names the same condition in both clauses, and the advance of al-Battānī’s apogee is given as 16;37° where the two longitudes on the page differ by 16;47°.

The seven discrepancies in reported figures are facts about the sources, not about Swerdlow’s page. Two are Ptolemy’s roundings of his mean motions. One is al-Battānī’s maximum equation as Regiomontanus transmits it, 1;59,10° against 1;59,9° from his eccentricity. Four belong to the determination of the parameters from the seasons: inverted exactly, the model gives e = 2;29,28 and λ_A = 65;35° from the two reported arcs, and e = 2;28,58 and λ_A = 65;26° from the day counts. Ptolemy’s 2;29,30 and 65;30° follow from his rounded arcs within the rounding of a chord computation, not from the day counts. The paragraph itself calls the derivation sensitive to errors in the intervals.
