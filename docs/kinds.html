Model, parametrisation, state

Three things are kept apart, because different things are true of each.

what it fixes where it stands what can be said of it
model the geometry: parameter symbols with units and admissible ranges, the kinematic variable, the construction, the observables a model text what holds for every admissible parameter value and every state
parametrisation the values of the parameters, each with source or gap, and whose values they are a parametrisation text what follows for these values: a maximum equation, a period
state the value of the kinematic variable, or a time an argument: at kappa_bar = 41° where everything is: a configuration

The model has no values

# Ptolemy's solar model in its eccentric form: the pure geometry.
# The description follows the use Swerdlow makes of the model in The Renaissance of Astronomy (2026), pp. 19–22
# (scan pages 49–52): the earth O, the centre E of the eccentric at the distance e in the direction of the
# apogee, the sun S moving uniformly about E. No value stands here; values are parametrisations.
model solar-eccentric "Ptolemy's solar model, eccentric form"
attributed "Ptolemy"
witness fr:kepler-sec/Swerdlow2026/0049/p04 "the construction, p. 19"
witness fr:kepler-sec/Swerdlow2026/0050/p03 "longitudes, anomalies and the equation, p. 20"
witness fr:kepler-sec/Swerdlow2026/0051/fig02 "Fig. 1.4"
author "Gerd Graßhoff"
tradition ptolemaic
frame geocentric

param R       : length "radius of the eccentric"                in (1, 1000)
param e       : length "eccentricity, the distance OE"          in (0, R / 2)
param lamA    : angle  "longitude of the apogee"
param T       : time   "tropical year"                          in (300, 400)
param kappa_0 : angle  "mean anomaly at t = 0"
display lamA "λ_A"
display kappa_0 "κ̄_0"

variable kappa_bar : angle "mean anomaly about E, counted from the apogee"
law kappa_bar = uniform(period: T, epoch: kappa_0) "the sun moves uniformly about E"
display kappa_bar "κ̄"

O   = observer                        "earth"
E   = O + polar(e, lamA)              "centre of the eccentric"
ecc = circle(E, R)                    "the eccentric"
A   = E + polar(R, lamA)              "apogee"
B   = E - polar(R, lamA)              "perigee"
S   = E + polar(R, lamA + kappa_bar)  "sun"
body sun at S

observe lam_bar = direction(E, S)     "mean longitude"
observe lam     = direction(O, S)     "true longitude"
observe kappa   = lam - lamA          "true anomaly about O"
observe c       = lam_bar - lam       "equation of centre, mean minus true longitude"
display lam_bar "λ̄_S"
display lam "λ_S"
display kappa "κ"

# stated propositions: checked over the parameter space and all states, not derived here
proposition p1: equal(lam_bar, lamA + kappa_bar) "the mean longitude is the longitude of the apogee plus the mean anomaly"
proposition p2: equal(kappa, kappa_bar - c)      "the true anomaly is the mean anomaly less the equation"
proposition p5: equal(lam, lam_bar - c)           "the true longitude is the mean longitude less the equation"
proposition p3: collinear(A, E, B)               "the apsidal line passes through E"
proposition p4: collinear(A, O, B)               "and through O"

A model text that writes param R = 60 … or gives a source to a value is refused, with a message that says where the value belongs. Because the model has no values, it is checked where it lives: over its parameter space (48 sampled points, each parameter in its range, ranges that depend on other parameters evaluated inside the sample) and its states (the kinematic variable through a revolution). A proposition stated in a model holds when it holds in every one of these configurations. equal(distance(O, E), R / 24) is true for Ptolemy’s values and is not a property of the model; stated in the model, it fails.

One model, several parametrisations

The pages give two sets of values for the eccentric form — Ptolemy’s and al-Battānī’s — and the printed figures use a third, an exaggerated eccentricity that makes the drawing legible.

Al-Battānī’s parametrisation is partial: the paragraph gives the eccentricity and the apogee, no year and no epoch. The check says so (complete fails), and what needs only R and e — the maximum equation — is computed with it all the same.

$ famulus-astra diff solar-eccentric.ptolemy solar-eccentric.battani
solar-eccentric.ptolemy ↔ solar-eccentric.battani
  values of Ptolemy ↔ al-Battānī
    R: 60 ↔ 60   (equal)
    e: 2;30 ↔ 2;4,45   (−0;25,15)
    lamA: 65;30° ↔ 82;17°   (+16;47)
    T: 365;14,48 ↔ —
    kappa_0: 0° ↔ —

The difference of the two apogees is 16;47°. The page prints 16;37° for it (Deep reading).

The kinematic variable and its law

variable kappa_bar : angle declares what varies. law kappa_bar = uniform(period: T, epoch: kappa_0) says how it runs in time, with the period and the epoch as ordinary parameters. A state names the variable directly (kappa_bar = 41°) or a time (t = 100 d), which the law turns into a value of the variable. A variable without a law is free; a figure of a model whose period has no value still moves, through the variable.

Drawing values are not attested values

A figure may be drawn with other values than the attested ones: drawn with e = 16. These are marked as drawing values in the figure text, in the figure’s corner and in its sheet, and they never touch the parametrisation. A figure without any parametrisation is drawn with drawing values only — the epicyclic form, to which the pages give no values of its own.
